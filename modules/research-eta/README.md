# Research ETA

Research ETA augments the game’s native Research pane by adding a compact Credit ETA indicator beside each Credit cost. The ETA text is slightly larger for readability.

Each available research card also receives a **Track** checkbox. Checking one item replaces the previous selection, saves the choice, and opens a Next-MCV-style Research Tracker with separate Credit and Research Point progress bars, remaining amounts, and the Credit ETA. The tracker refreshes from live player resources every five seconds and can also be opened from the normal Research ETA module shortcut.

The selected research item and the tracker's open/closed state persist through a game refresh. If the tracker was open, it reopens with the same item and its saved window position; if it was closed, it stays closed. To reopen it without changing the selection, use **Research Center** in the Suite Base Tools dock or the **Research ETA** shortcut in the normal Suite module controls.

It does not change research prerequisites, costs, or actions. Tracking is read-only and the game’s normal Research button remains the only way to begin research.
