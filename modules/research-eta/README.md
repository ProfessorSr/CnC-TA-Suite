# Research ETA

Research ETA owns and opens a separate CnC-TA-Suite window. It does not open,
modify, inspect, decorate, rename, or embed the game's original Research pane.

The custom window reads research and resource state from the game model and
reproduces faction-specific GDI and NOD cards, tabs, paging, prerequisites,
costs, and actions with Suite-owned artwork. It shows current, required, and
remaining resource progress plus compact production ETAs where a growth rate is
available. Research actions use the game model and do not delegate to the
original Research pane or its controls.
